ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK (2026)

1. ATTENDANCE
Students and trainees must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each semester.
Employees must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% of scheduled working days each quarter.
Anyone falling below 75% attendance receives a written warning. A second consecutive
quarter below 75% may lead to probation.

2. WORK FROM HOME
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Remote work on Mondays and Fridays
requires manager approval. All remote work must be logged in the attendance portal
before 10 AM.

3. LEAVE POLICY
Every employee is entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Sick leave up to 12 days per year is granted with a medical certificate for absences
longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused annual leave up to 5 days can be carried over
to the next year.

4. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday through Friday. A lunch break of
one hour is included. Overtime beyond 45 hours per week is compensated at 1.5x
the hourly rate.

5. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Travel expenses up to $150 per day are reimbursed with receipts. Meal expenses during
business travel are capped at $60 per day. Reimbursement requests must be submitted
within 30 days of the expense.

6. PROBATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. After confirmation, the notice
period for resignation is 30 days. During probation, the notice period is 7 days.

7. DATA SECURITY
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and rotated every 90 days.
Confidential documents must never be shared over personal email.
